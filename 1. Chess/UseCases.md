## UC-1: Play a Game of Chess
### Background information:
- **System:** chess simulation
- **Primary actor:** chess player
- **Secondary actor:** opponent player
- **Goal:** play a game of chess
- **Preconditions:** both players are ready to play
### Scenarios:
- **Basic flow:** User starts a game. Players are assigned white and black pieces randomly. Player with white pieces makes first move. Each player takes turn and makes a move until the end of the game is reached
- **Alternate flow 1:** User starts a game. One of the players leaves the simulation. Game ends with a defeat for the player who left.
- **Alternate flow 2:** User starts a game. One of the players resigns. Game ends with a defeat for the player who resigned.
